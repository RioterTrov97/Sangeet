# sangeet

## 0.3.0

### Minor Changes

- 8ec2c4b: When signed in, the avatar button and the account menu now show your YouTube Music photo and name, and Settings shows them too.
- 8ec2c4b: When signed in, History and Home's Recently played now include what you played on your other devices; History has "All devices" and "This phone" chips.
- 8ec2c4b: New "Your Recap" screen (account menu, when signed in) with a Play your Recap button and your YouTube Music Recap shelves.
- 8ec2c4b: When signed in, songs you play are now added to your YouTube history by default, so YouTube Music learns your taste for Quick picks and mixes. You can turn this off in Settings.
- 8ec2c4b: When signed in, For you now shows your own YouTube Music picks: up to 20 Quick picks, your mixes, Listen again and more, plus a Your Supermix button and your playlists and liked songs.
- 8ec2c4b: When signed in, "Add to playlist" now lists your YouTube Music playlists and can create a new one on YouTube Music or on this phone.

### Patch Changes

- 8ec2c4b: "New from artists you follow" covers up to 16 artists when signed in (your YouTube subscriptions included), and Recently played shows on a fresh signed-in phone.
- 8ec2c4b: Signing in to YouTube Music now actually works on iPhone: your Home, likes, history and photo load from your account. If you signed in before, sign in once more when the app asks.

## 0.2.0

### Minor Changes

- 4697705: Android Auto search: say or (when parked) type a search in the car to get songs, albums and artists, or say "Hey Google, play … on Sangeet". Your recent searches show up in the car too.
- 4697705: Sangeet now runs on Android phones. The same APK as the TV shows the phone app, with media notification and lock-screen controls, Material icons, songs from this phone, links shared from YouTube, YouTube Music and Spotify, the phone as a remote for the TV, in-app updates, and Android Auto.
- 4697705: The Android app now updates itself from GitHub Releases: a pill on Home and Settings > Updates offer new versions, and with automatic updates on it installs them when nothing is playing.
