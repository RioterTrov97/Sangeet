Sangeet is a music player for your iPhone. Search for songs, albums, artists and playlists, play them in the background, and keep the ones you love for offline listening.

Playing
- Home shelves, mood pages and search with suggestions
- Now Playing with a queue you can reorder, shuffle and repeat
- Synced lyrics that follow the song, from several lyrics sources, with a sync control when a line is early or late
- Song and Video modes: switch to the music video when one exists, with full-screen landscape
- Lock screen and Control Center controls

Your library
- Like songs, save albums, follow artists and make your own playlists
- Download songs and albums to listen without a connection; recently played songs are cached automatically within a limit you choose
- Songs from this iPhone: import audio files from the Files app, or drop them into the Sangeet folder, and they play like any other song
- Import a public Spotify playlist or album and match it to songs you can play

Accounts and privacy
- You can search and play without signing in
- Signing in with your YouTube Music account (through the sign-in page itself, never through a password field in the app) adds your own library and recommendations
- No ads, no analytics and no tracking in the app

Also
- Control Sangeet on a Google TV from your phone (the TV app is a separate install)
- An optional backup server that you host yourself can be added in Settings

Sangeet is an independent app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google. Music is played from sources you can already reach, and availability depends on them.
